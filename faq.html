Getting Started
How do I create an account on 3Stars Entertainers?

Click the Sign Up button at the top right of any page. Fill in your name, email address, and a secure password. Verify your email via the link we send you, and your account will be ready to use instantly.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial on all new subscriptions. You can explore our full karaoke library, download sample tracks, and test all premium features before committing to a paid plan.

What devices are supported by your platform?

Our platform works on:

  • Desktop & laptop (Windows, macOS, Linux)
  • Smartphones & tablets (Android & iOS)
  • Smart TVs via our mobile app casting

For the best experience, we recommend using Chrome or Firefox on desktop, and our official mobile app on Android/iOS.

Technical Issues
The audio and lyrics are out of sync. How do I fix it?

Try these steps:

  • Refresh the page or restart the app.
  • Clear your browser cache or app data.
  • Ensure a stable internet connection.
  • Use a wired connection if on desktop.

If the issue persists, please report the issue with the song name.

I can't log in to my account. What should I do?

Use the Forgot Password link on the login page to reset. If you still can't log in, contact us at support@3stars.com with your registered email.

Why is my download failing?

Common causes include insufficient storage, unstable internet, or a full download queue. Free up storage space and retry. If the problem continues, report the issue with the song name and error message.
