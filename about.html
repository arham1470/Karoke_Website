Our story

We are the best karaoke specialists

3Stars Entertainers

Karaoke specialists · Since 2023

For about three years, we've built a reputation as one of the most trusted karaoke YouTube channels — winning the hearts of music fans by providing free, high-quality tracks of famous Tamil songs, complete with fully-synced lyrics videos.

We have now decided to take that mission further by building a dedicated platform where music lovers can browse, preview, and download karaoke tracks — with better quality, better search, and better support than anywhere else.

Our community of 20,000+ subscribers and 5 million viewers worldwide continues to grow every day. To everyone who has supported us, purchased our tracks, or simply shared our channel — thank you. You are the reason this platform exists.
